This is a soundtrack album created based on the visual artwork "New Century Underwater Cavern". As global warming gradually raises sea levels, the coming of a future water world will submerge human civilization remnants, which will dissolve into cables of various colors. Tens of thousands of humans will flow through these cables in the form of bytes, moving at the speed of light, blinking into the ocean floor to form caverns that emit shimmering lights. Apostle Seven is born...
